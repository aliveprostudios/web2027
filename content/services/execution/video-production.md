## Stories That Sell

85% of people say they’ve been convinced to buy a product or service by watching a video. But there’s a difference between a video that exists and a video that performs. The difference is strategy.

Most businesses hire a production crew, shoot something that looks polished, and drop it online with no connection to their brand positioning, their messaging, their audience, or their sales funnel. The video looks professional. But it doesn’t work hard enough because it was built in isolation.

> *“A video without strategy is just footage with a logo on it. When we produce a film for a client, the script comes from their messaging, the visuals follow their brand system, and the distribution connects to their campaign. That’s why our videos perform. They’re not standalone content. They’re strategic assets.”*
