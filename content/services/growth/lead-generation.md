## Leads That Actually Convert

Lead generation isn’t a service. It’s the result of everything else working together.

Think about it. A lead doesn’t appear out of thin air. Someone saw your ad, your content, or your social presence. They clicked. They landed on a page. They read your messaging. They decided you were worth their contact information. Every one of those moments was shaped by your brand, your strategy, your design, your copy, your funnel, and your campaign. If any of those elements are weak, disconnected, or off-brand, the lead either never arrives or never converts.

**65% of a company’s business comes from existing customers, yet 44% of companies focus more on acquisition than retention. Are you generating the right leads, or just more leads?**
