## From Stranger to Customer

Where do your leads go after they click? After they visit your website, download your guide, or engage with your ad?

If the answer is “we’re not sure,” you don’t have a sales funnel. You have a leak.

Most businesses spend real money driving traffic but have no structured system for what happens next. Visitors arrive, look around, and leave. Leads come in and go cold. The marketing generates activity, but the activity doesn’t convert because there’s no designed path from first impression to closed deal.

**How many leads entered your pipeline last month? How many converted? If you can’t answer both questions instantly, your funnel needs work.**

## How We Build Your Funnel

### Top of Funnel: Awareness.

We design entry points that attract the right people through content, social media, search, and paid campaigns. The goal is to capture attention, earn the first click, and begin prequalifying prospects from the very first interaction.

### Middle of Funnel: Nurture.

This is where most businesses lose their leads. We build the nurture layer with strategic content, email sequences, retargeting, and value-driven touchpoints that keep your brand present and progressively build trust. We make sure the right type of material is in place at each stage of the sales cycle.

### Bottom of Funnel: Conversion.

This is where qualified leads convert. We design conversion-stage assets like case studies, demos, consultations, and high-converting landing pages. By this point, leads have been prequalified and qualified through the funnel, so your sales team is spending time on real opportunities, not cold contacts.

## The Infrastructure Behind the Funnel

### Tracking & Data Collection.

Implementation of tracking codes, pixels, and event tags across your website and landing pages so every interaction is captured and measurable.

### CRM Setup & Integration.

If you need a CRM or your current one isn’t doing its job, we implement and configure the right system to manage leads, track pipeline activity, and ensure nothing falls through the cracks.

### Lead Prequalification & Qualification.

We build scoring and filtering mechanisms into the funnel so your sales team receives leads that have been vetted, not just collected.

### Sales Team Readiness.

We make sure your team is trained, equipped with the right materials, and ready to manage sales capacity with the care and follow-through that converts.

### Stage-Specific Sales Materials.

We create and align the right content, collateral, and assets for awareness, consideration, and decision stages.

- Funnel strategy and architecture mapped to your buyer journey
- High-converting landing pages designed within your brand system
- Email sequences and automated nurture workflows
- Lead magnets (guides, checklists, assessments, tools)
- Tracking implementation and analytics setup

- Lead scoring and qualification frameworks

- Ongoing funnel optimization based on performance data
