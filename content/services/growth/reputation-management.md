## Control the Narrative

What shows up when someone searches your company name? Whatever they find, that’s your reputation.

Not what you think it is. What Google, social media, and review platforms say it is.

Most businesses don’t think about reputation management until something goes wrong. A negative review surfaces. A competitor’s content outranks your own brand name. By then, the damage is already shaping how prospects perceive you before your sales team ever gets a chance to speak.

**Over 90% of consumers read online reviews before making a purchasing decision. What are they reading about you?**

### The Audit: Know Where You Stand

We start with a deep-level audit across search engines, social media, review sites, and directories to uncover exactly what’s being said about your business. The audit gives you three things:

- A clear snapshot of your online reputation today, including what you would rather not see
- Critical issues that need to be addressed, corrected, or removed
- Opportunities your competitors haven’t discovered or acted on

You can’t manage what you can’t see. The audit makes everything visible.

### Search Engine Reputation

When a prospect searches your brand name, the first page of results tells a story. Is it yours, or someone else’s? We optimize your search presence so the content ranking for your brand name is content you control: your website, your social profiles, your published content, and your review platforms. This connects directly to your SEO and content marketing strategy.

### Google Review System

Online reviews are one of the most influential factors in buying decisions. We implement a system-based process that makes it easy for your business to request, collect, and publish Google reviews from your satisfied clients on a consistent basis. This isn’t a one-time ask. It’s a structured, repeatable workflow that builds your review volume steadily over time, strengthens your local search rankings, and creates a growing library of social proof.

We also set up monitoring so you know the moment a new review appears, and we help you develop a professional response framework for handling both positive and negative feedback.

**What Our Reputation Management Includes**

- Comprehensive online reputation audit (search, social, reviews, directories)
- Search result optimization for brand-related queries
- Google review system implementation (request, collection, and publishing workflow)
- Review monitoring and professional response framework
- Negative content suppression through strategic SEO
- Social media profile optimization and monitoring
- Directory and listing management for accuracy and consistency
- Ongoing monitoring and reporting

Your prospects are researching you right now. Let’s make sure what they find matches the quality of what you deliver.
