## 2. The pain, named

**H2:** One brand. Six briefs. Six versions of you.

How many vendors does it take to run your brand? One for strategy. One for design. One for
the website. One for video. One for social. One for ads. And somehow you are the one
making sure they all tell the same story.

- Your website says one thing. Your sales deck says another.
- Every vendor optimizes their own slice. Nobody owns the result.
- Budget leaks in the gaps between them.
- You become the project manager for your own brand.

That is not a marketing problem. It is a structural one, and no amount of spend fixes it.

*Source: the Why Alive Pro opening, tightened.*

## 3. The answer

**H2:** One partner. One strategy. Every capability under one roof.

Alive ProStudios was founded in Toronto in 1997 to do what most agencies structurally
cannot: handle brand, marketing, creative, and technology with one team, working from one
strategy.

Not because being the biggest was the goal. Because client after client, year after year,
we watched fragmented marketing produce fragmented results. The businesses that grow
fastest are the ones where strategy, identity, voice, design, content, campaigns, and
systems are synchronized. We build that synchronization.
