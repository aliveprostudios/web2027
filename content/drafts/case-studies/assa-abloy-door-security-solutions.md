## We Are Not Their Marketing Department

ASSA ABLOY Door Security Solutions is the Canadian door and access security division of ASSA ABLOY, the international group that is the largest access solutions business in the world. It runs a fully established internal design and marketing department, and that team is good at its job.

A company of that size does not go looking for an agency to take the function over, which makes this relationship the opposite of most of our work. What a team like that needs is somewhere to escalate to when a project outruns internal capacity, calls for production they do not do day to day, or lands with a deadline that cannot be moved.

We are the extended arm for exactly those moments, and the measure of the work is that their team never has to explain a miss.

***

## Cinematic Video Production.

We produce and script video for major digital rollouts, including their Webshop 2.0 platform launch. A platform launch is the hardest kind of video to get right, because the subject is a system rather than an object and the audience has to understand what changed for them specifically. Scripting carries most of that weight, which is why we write before anyone picks up a camera.

## Event Photography.

Corporate events are photographed to record scale and to record atmosphere, and most coverage manages only the first. We shoot for both, because the images have to work as a record afterwards and as the argument for attending next time.

## Campaign Design.

For specific campaigns we build the visual structure and the assets that sit inside it, working from the group's brand system rather than around it.

## French Localization, at Speed.

Urgent material needs accurate French faster than internal processes can turn it around. We handle that, which is less a translation service than a requirement of operating properly in this country. A Canadian campaign that ships in one language has not shipped.

## The Relief Valve.

The rest is overflow: the urgent project that arrives in a week already full, absorbed without the brand drifting and without the internal team reorganizing around it.

## Reading From a Brand Core We Did Not Write.

Most of our work begins by defining a client's Brand Core, the strategic truth every other decision reads from. Here it was defined long ago, at a global head office, and it is not ours to touch.

The [Brand-to-Revenue Performance System](/alive-pro/our-system) still applies, which is the point worth making. We operate almost entirely inside Execution, drawing on a Foundation that belongs to someone else, and the discipline is unchanged: every asset reads from one source of truth. The only difference is who wrote it.

That is why the work has to be indistinguishable from everything else the brand produces anywhere in the world. Creative that announces its authorship has failed the brief, however good it is. Global systems earn their value precisely because nobody in any single market improvises with them.

## What It Actually Buys Them.

An internal team brings in a partner because it is short of hours, not short of ideas. So the value is not creative direction. It is that a marketing manager with a full calendar does not have to spend an afternoon briefing an agency on things the agency should already know, and does not have to check whether the work will come back on brand.

Being trusted inside a global marketing organization is a slower achievement than winning a pitch. It is granted on the evidence of work that never created a problem.
