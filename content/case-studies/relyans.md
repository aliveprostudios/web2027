## Custom software development for industrial manufacturing.

Relyans approached Alive ProStudios to develop a new estimate generator for its manufacturing operations. Our discovery process revealed a broader opportunity to simplify complex workflows, connect departments and strengthen operational and commercial control. The result was Relyans CORE, a proprietary, web-based operational excellence platform designed and developed around the needs of the business.

### About Relyans.

Relyans is a global supplier of engineered fastening solutions, specializing in ultra-high-tensile fasteners. It serves automotive and commercial vehicle manufacturers, Tier 1 suppliers, and businesses in agriculture, eMobility, new energy and industrial manufacturing.

Its expertise spans product engineering, application development, rapid prototyping, cold forming and advanced testing. With manufacturing operations in Michigan and Mexico, Relyans supports demanding applications ranging from powertrain and suspension components to braking and occupant safety systems.

## Assessment and strategic direction.

### The existing estimating environment.

The estimating process relied on Excel, fragmented information and manual coordination. Preparing or revising an estimate required individuals across departments to collect, reconcile and consolidate information from different sources.

A customer request may begin with a detailed technical drawing. Translating its specifications into a manufacturing estimate requires consideration of raw materials, equipment capabilities, labour, production processes and commercial variables. These factors are interconnected, and changes to one can have implications elsewhere in the estimate.

### A broader operational strategy.

Rather than reproducing the existing process in a new application, we challenged the original requirements. Another estimate generator could improve an individual task without resolving the broader challenges surrounding it.

We examined how sales, estimating, engineering, production, plant management and leadership interact, identifying opportunities to connect their responsibilities, information and decisions within a common operational environment. The objective was to give the organization greater visibility and flexibility throughout the estimating process, not simply to automate calculations.

Technology was the enabler of this broader strategy, not its starting point.

## Simplification of complex business processes.

### Understanding the business before engineering the solution.

Relyans CORE represents one of the most complex software systems Alive ProStudios has engineered. The challenge was to preserve the sophistication required by industrial costing while making the experience intuitive for the people using the platform.

Simplifying complexity is central to our methodology. We explore the core DNA of a business, dissect its processes, examine interdependencies and extract essential business logic. By understanding why processes exist and how they relate to one another, we can distinguish necessary complexity from avoidable operational friction.

### Designing an intuitive user experience.

We organized the underlying business logic into interconnected components and designed the user experience around the responsibilities of each team. Users can focus on their work without having to navigate every calculation, dependency or technical process themselves.

This approach also supports adaptability. Understanding the relationships between processes before engineering the system helps establish an architecture that can evolve without unnecessary rework.

Complexity should reside within the engineering, not within the user experience.

## Design and development of Relyans CORE.

### A unified operational platform.

Alive ProStudios conceptualized, architected, designed and custom-developed Relyans CORE as a dynamic operational excellence platform. Its architecture connects centralized information, intelligent costing, collaborative workflows, configurable permissions and business insights within a web-based environment.

At its centre is a proprietary costing engine that brings technical, operational and commercial variables into a structured estimating experience. Its components work together while preserving the distinct responsibilities of the teams involved.

![A network of interconnected data models across intake, engineering, the cost engine, commercial functions and governance.](/assets/case-studies/relyans/body-02.png)

### Integration of information across the estimating workflow.

Customer records, master data, materials, equipment information, vendor quotations and pricing rules contribute to the estimating workflow at relevant stages. The platform brings these inputs together within a structured process that supports review, approval and the locking of approved estimates.

The integration simplifies the management of interconnected information while supporting consistency and traceability. The proprietary calculation methodology remains protected.

![Information streams converge across the estimating workflow, through the calculation core and into a locked approved estimate.](/assets/case-studies/relyans/body-03.png)

### Departmental collaboration and centralized information.

One of the defining objectives was to bring the teams involved in estimating together. Authorized users can access relevant information, leave contextual comments and participate in review and approval workflows directly within the platform.

Embedding collaboration into the work itself reduces the need for separate email exchanges and manual consolidation of information. It also helps maintain continuity and accountability as estimates move between departments.

### Configurable roles and business intelligence.

Bringing an organization together does not mean giving everyone access to everything. Relyans CORE incorporates configurable roles and permissions that align information, functionality and authority with individual responsibilities. Authorized administrators can adapt these settings as organizational needs evolve.

Role-based dashboards extend this principle to business intelligence. Leadership, sales, operations and other authorized users can access insights relevant to their respective responsibilities, supporting informed decisions without introducing unnecessary information into their working environment.

### Commercial flexibility and control.

Material costs, transportation expenses, tariffs and production requirements can change. Relyans CORE gives authorized users granular control over relevant costing and pricing variables, allowing targeted adjustments without unnecessarily rebuilding estimates.

Even subtle adjustments can influence a commercial outcome. Giving teams the flexibility to evaluate and manage those changes supports competitive pricing decisions, margin protection and opportunities for greater profitability.

## Architecture, quality assurance and lifecycle support.

### Connected platform services.

Beyond the visible application, supporting services bring together identity, data management, audit trails, document output, communication, storage and monitoring. These components underpin the operational experience and support the management of the platform over time.

Automated testing, delivery pipelines and specialist AI agents support the engineering and review process. These development capabilities are distinct from the operational functionality available to business users.

![The CORE platform surrounded by foundation, service and delivery rings showing connected services, data, identity, audit trails, testing, pipelines and development agents.](/assets/case-studies/relyans/body-04.png)

### Scalability and operational independence.

Relyans CORE was engineered to accommodate evolving business requirements, additional equipment, manufacturing facilities and changing organizational responsibilities. Its configurable architecture allows authorized users to manage routine operational changes without unnecessary reliance on external developers.

The objective was to establish a platform that can evolve with the business rather than a fixed application that must be reinvented as requirements change.

### Implementation and ongoing support.

Our involvement spanned discovery, solution architecture, user experience design, custom software development, testing, refinement, deployment and ongoing support.

Throughout development, we proactively identified opportunities to introduce functionality beyond the original requirements. We treated Relyans CORE as if it were our own platform, bringing a sense of ownership, responsiveness and commitment to continuous improvement.

A dedicated Help Centre provides guidance for users, supporting knowledge transfer, adoption and the independent resolution of routine questions.

## Operational and commercial outcomes.

Relyans CORE replaces a fragmented estimating environment with a connected platform for the teams, information and workflows involved in industrial costing. Centralized data, contextual collaboration and configurable controls support greater accuracy, operational consistency and organizational alignment.

The commercial significance extends beyond estimating. When teams can access relevant information, coordinate decisions and make targeted adjustments without unnecessary administrative work, they are better positioned to manage resources, respond to changing conditions and protect margins.

Efficiency and productivity create a stronger foundation for profitability.

## Unified digital infrastructure for manufacturing.

Advances in artificial intelligence, intelligent automation and cloud technologies give manufacturers an opportunity to reconsider how their digital infrastructure is organized. Disconnected applications may serve individual functions while collectively introducing fragmented information, redundant workflows and operational dependencies.

Purpose-built software can help reduce unnecessary fragmentation, connect essential systems and establish a stronger foundation for introducing emerging technologies where they deliver meaningful value. The objective is not to replace every specialized application, but to retain or integrate the technologies the business needs within a coherent operational environment.
