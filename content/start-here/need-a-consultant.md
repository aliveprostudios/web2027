## You Probably Already Know

Most people who ask for an outside opinion are not lost. They have a suspicion they cannot act on, usually because everybody they could ask about it has a stake in the answer.

That is the real value of an outsider, and it is not expertise. It is the absence of a position to defend. Your team cannot tell you the positioning is wrong without implying the last two years were wasted. Your agency cannot tell you the channel is wrong without cutting its own retainer. We have neither problem, so we can say the thing plainly.

What we do is look at the whole business rather than the piece you asked about: how you are positioned against the alternatives, what the market actually believes about you, and where the money is leaking between the two. Sometimes that produces a project. Often it produces a short list of things to stop doing, which costs you nothing and is worth more.

> *"The most useful thing a consultant can say is that the problem is not the one you hired them to solve. That sentence is worth more than the engagement, and most people will not say it."*

**Javad Ahmadi, Brand Transformation Architect**

Bring us the thing you already suspect is wrong. An hour is usually enough to tell whether your instinct is right, and whether fixing it needs us or not.
