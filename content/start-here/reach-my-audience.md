## Ideal Is Not a Demographic

Reaching the right people is a targeting problem only after it is a definition problem. Most companies can describe their ideal customer in categories. Very few can describe the moment that customer starts looking.

That moment is the whole game. Nobody wakes up wanting what you sell. Something happens first: a supplier fails, a contract comes up for renewal, a regulation changes, they lose a bid they should have won. If you know the trigger, you can be there when it fires. If all you know is an industry and a job title, you are buying attention and hoping some of it lands near someone who cares.

So we work out who actually buys, what moved them when they moved, and what they typed or asked at that moment. Then we make sure you are the answer they find, whether the question goes to Google or to an AI assistant, which is increasingly where it gets asked first.

> *"Everybody can name their target market. Almost nobody can name the week their customer decides. That week is where the budget should go."*

**Javad Ahmadi, Brand Transformation Architect**

Tell us about the last three customers you won and what was happening in their business when they came looking. The pattern is usually visible straight away.
