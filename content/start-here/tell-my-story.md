## The Story Is Fine. Nobody Wrote It Down.

Most companies asking for content do not have a story problem. They have the story. It comes out properly when the founder tells it in a room, and it thins out the moment somebody else has to write it.

That is why content made at volume so often feels hollow. It is not badly produced. It is made without the thing the founder says in the third minute of a meeting, the specific detail about how the work is really done, because nobody ever captured it. Every supplier since has been paraphrasing a paraphrase, and each pass loses a little more of what made it true.

We start by getting the real version out of the people who carry it, then build the video, the photography and the writing on top of that rather than around it. Authentic is not a tone of voice you can specify in a brief. It is the difference between content made from what your business actually does and content made from a description of it.

> *"Every business has one paragraph only the founder can say properly. Get that paragraph and the rest of the content writes itself. Miss it, and no amount of production saves the work."*

**Javad Ahmadi, Brand Transformation Architect**

Talk us through what you do as if we were a customer rather than a supplier. That conversation is usually the raw material, and it is usually enough.
