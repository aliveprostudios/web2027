## You Are Selling the Wrong Thing Well

When a good product markets badly, the marketing is rarely the first failure. Usually the product's real advantage has never been put into words, so the marketing does a competent job of selling something else.

You can hear it in how the company describes itself. The feature list is accurate and it is not why anybody bought. Ask your best customers why they chose you and the answer tends to be something about how you work rather than what you make, and it appears nowhere in your materials. That gap is the problem. Adding more marketing on top of it just distributes the wrong message more efficiently.

So we find the real reason first, from the people who already paid for it. Positioning, messaging and campaigns get built on that answer, which is the point where marketing stops feeling like a translation of the business and starts sounding like it.

> *"If your marketing feels off, do not start with the marketing. Ask five customers why they chose you. The answer is almost never on your website, and it is always the thing you should have been saying."*

**Javad Ahmadi, Brand Transformation Architect**

Tell us what your best customer said when you asked why they picked you. If you have never asked, that is where we would start, and it costs nothing.
