# BRAND PULSE

Five questions, two minutes, one honest read on how alive your brand really is. Answer the way things are today, not the way you want them to be.

Start button: **Take the Pulse**

## Questions (5)

### 1. When someone asks what your company does, how consistent is the answer?

- Everyone explains it differently *(0 pts)*
- Close, but the wording drifts *(7 pts)*
- Mostly consistent across the team *(14 pts)*
- One sharp sentence, told the same way by everyone *(20 pts)*

### 2. Put your visual identity beside your strongest competitor. What happens?

- We look dated next to them *(0 pts)*
- We blend in with the category *(7 pts)*
- We hold our own *(14 pts)*
- We are clearly the more commanding brand *(20 pts)*

### 3. How often does your brand publish something a prospect would remember?

- Rarely, and only when someone finds time *(0 pts)*
- Occasionally, without a plan *(7 pts)*
- On a steady schedule *(14 pts)*
- Consistently, with a strategy behind every piece *(20 pts)*

### 4. Where do new clients actually come from today?

- Referrals only, and they are slowing *(0 pts)*
- Referrals plus the occasional lucky find *(7 pts)*
- A mix of referrals and steady inbound interest *(14 pts)*
- A predictable pipeline our brand keeps filling *(20 pts)*

### 5. What happens after someone lands on your website?

- Honestly, we would rather they did not see it *(0 pts)*
- They get the basics, then leave *(7 pts)*
- They stay a while and sometimes reach out *(14 pts)*
- It sells for us: visitors become conversations *(20 pts)*
