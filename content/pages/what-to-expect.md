## Before You Call, Here’s How It Works

Choosing a branding and marketing partner is a significant decision. You’re not just buying a service. You’re trusting someone with the way your business shows up in the market.

We understand that, and we want you to know exactly what the experience looks like before you take the first step.

### The Initial Conversation

It starts with a call. No pitch. No pressure. No generic presentation. We use this first conversation to listen. We want to understand your business, your goals, your challenges, and what’s prompted you to look for a partner right now.

Come prepared to talk about what’s working, what isn’t, and where you want to be. The more honest the conversation, the more useful it is for both of us. By the end of this call, you’ll know whether there’s a fit, and so will we. Not every business is the right match for how we work, and we’d rather be upfront about that than waste your time.

### Discovery & Audit

If there’s a fit, we go deeper. We conduct a thorough discovery process to understand your current brand, your market, your competition, and your existing marketing efforts. Depending on the scope, this may include a competitive intelligence audit, a review of your digital presence, an assessment of your sales process, and conversations with your leadership team.

This phase gives us the clarity to build a strategy that’s grounded in reality, not assumptions. It also gives you an honest, clear-eyed picture of where your brand stands today and where the opportunities are.

### Strategy & Proposal

Based on what we learn in discovery, we build a customized proposal. Not a template. Not a menu of services with price tags. A strategic recommendation that maps the specific work your business needs to the outcomes you want to achieve.

Every proposal is structured around our four-phase Brand Transformation System (Foundation, Execution, Growth, Leadership), tailored to where you are in your brand journey. Some clients need the full system. Others need to start with one phase. We meet you where you are and build a roadmap from there.

### How We Work Together

Once the engagement begins, here’s what the working relationship looks like:

- A dedicated team. You work with the same people throughout. No revolving door of junior staff.
- Clear communication rhythm. Regular check-ins, milestone reviews, and transparent updates.
- Collaborative approvals. We present. You review. We refine. Structured and efficient.
- Defined timelines. Every phase has clear milestones and delivery dates.

> *“The best client relationships we have are the ones where both sides feel like they’re on the same team. That’s not something you manufacture. It’s something you earn through transparency, accountability, and consistently doing what you said you’d do.”*

**Javad Ahmadi,** **Brand Transformation Architect**

### How We Measure Success

We don’t measure success by how many deliverables we produce. We measure it by what those deliverables achieve. Every engagement is tied to defined goals and KPIs established during the strategy phase. You’ll receive regular reporting that focuses on outcomes, not activity. We want you to see the return on your investment clearly and confidently.

### Not Ready for a Full Engagement? Start With a Sprint.

If you’re not sure where to start, or if you want to validate your current marketing before committing to a larger engagement, our Precision Impact Sprint is designed for exactly that. It’s a focused 90-minute strategy session that pinpoints what’s working, identifies what’s leaking budget, and gives you a clear action plan you can execute immediately. Many of our longest client relationships started with a Sprint.

Working with Alive Pro should feel like gaining a strategic advantage, not adding another vendor to manage. If you’re ready to have that first conversation, let’s talk.
