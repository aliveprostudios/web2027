# Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 19, 2026

This policy explains what information Alive ProStudios Inc. collects about you, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to aliveprostudios.com and any services we provide directly.

## 1. Who we are

Alive ProStudios Inc. is a brand transformation firm based in Toronto, Canada. We work with clients across Canada, the United States, Germany, and the Middle East. We are the data controller for personal information collected through this site.

You can reach our privacy team at privacy@aliveprostudios.com .

## 2. Information we collect

We collect only the information we need to respond to you and to operate this site.

- Contact form submissions: name, email address, optional phone number, optional company name, optional service category, and the message you send. The page you submitted from and the language locale are also recorded so we can route the enquiry correctly.

- Analytics data (only with your consent): pages visited, approximate location at country level, device and browser type, referring site, and time on page. We use this only to understand how the site is used and to improve content and performance.

- Technical access logs: our hosting provider records standard server access logs (IP address, request path, timestamp, response status) for security and reliability purposes. These are retained for a short period and are not used to build advertising profiles.

## 3. How we use your information

- To respond to enquiries you send us through the contact form and to follow up about the services you asked about.

- To send you communications you have specifically requested, such as our brochure or follow-up materials.

- To understand site usage in aggregate so we can improve content, navigation, and performance.

- To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents.

- To comply with legal obligations that apply to our business.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data to train AI models. We do not use it for advertising targeting outside of our own follow-up communications.

## 4. Cookies and analytics

When you arrive on the site, you will see a cookie banner asking whether you accept analytics cookies. Until you click Accept, no analytics scripts are loaded and no analytics cookies are set. If you decline or simply ignore the banner, you can continue to browse without any tracking.

If you accept, we use Google Analytics 4 to collect aggregated usage statistics. Your choice is stored locally in your browser (in localStorage under the key cookie-consent ) and is remembered on this device. You can change your mind at any time by clicking the “Cookie settings” link in the footer of any page, which re-opens the banner so you can accept or decline.

Some essential, non-tracking cookies may still be used by our hosting platform for load balancing and security. These do not identify you personally.

## 5. Service providers we use

We rely on a small number of trusted vendors to operate the site. They process personal information only on our behalf and under contracts that limit how they can use it.

- Vercel Inc. (United States): hosting, content delivery, and server access logs.

- Supabase Inc. (United States): database storage for contact form submissions.

- Resend (United States): transactional email delivery for contact form notifications.

- Sanity.io (United States): content management system. Sanity stores site content, not visitor data.

- Google Analytics (United States, only with your consent): aggregated traffic analytics.

- YouTube and Vimeo (United States, only when you accept cookies or click to load): embedded video playback on certain pages. These providers may set cookies and collect playback data.

Because these providers are based in the United States, your data may be transferred outside Canada and the European Economic Area. We rely on standard contractual safeguards required under the GDPR and PIPEDA for these transfers.

## 6. When we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone. We share it only with the service providers listed above for the purposes described, or when we are legally required to (for example, in response to a valid court order or regulatory request).

If our business is ever involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to the same protections described here.

## 7. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights regarding the personal information we hold about you:

- Access: request a copy of what we have.

- Correction: ask us to fix information that is inaccurate or incomplete.

- Deletion: ask us to delete your information (subject to legal retention requirements).

- Withdraw consent: opt out of analytics at any time, or unsubscribe from any follow-up communications.

- Data portability: receive a copy of your data in a portable format.

- Complain: lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, your province's privacy regulator, or your EU member state authority).

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@aliveprostudios.com . We respond within thirty days.

## 8. Canadian anti-spam law (CASL)

When you submit our contact form, you are giving us express consent to reply to your enquiry. Any further commercial email you receive from us will identify the sender, include a clear unsubscribe mechanism, and honour your unsubscribe request promptly. Submitting the form is not consent to be added to a marketing list.

## 9. How long we keep your data

- Contact form submissions are kept for as long as we have an ongoing client relationship with you, and for up to seven years afterwards for accounting and dispute resolution. You may ask us to delete sooner.

- Analytics data is retained at the default retention setting of our analytics provider (typically fourteen months).

- Server access logs are retained for thirty days for security and operations.

## 10. Children

This site is intended for business audiences and is not directed at children under sixteen. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please email us and we will delete it.

## 11. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. This includes encryption in transit, role-based access controls on our database, and limited internal access on a need-to-know basis. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the date at the top of the page. If we make a material change that affects how we use your information, we will provide reasonable notice on the site before the change takes effect.
