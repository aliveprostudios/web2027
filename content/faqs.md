## About Alive ProStudios

### What services does Alive ProStudios offer?

We offer full-spectrum brand services across four domains: Foundation (brand strategy, identity, voice, rebranding), Execution (design, web, video, photography, social branding), Growth (marketing, SEO, AEO, lead generation, content marketing), and Infrastructure (solution architecture, app development, systems integration, dashboards).

### What industries do you work with?

We work with businesses across many industries including technology, professional services, healthcare, real estate, retail, and manufacturing. Our approach is rooted in brand strategy principles that apply across sectors.

### Where is Alive ProStudios located?

We are based in Toronto, Canada (Greater Toronto Area) and serve clients across Canada, the United States, Germany, and the Middle East. We work both in-person and remotely.

### How long has Alive ProStudios been in business?

Alive ProStudios was founded in 1997. We have 29 years of experience in brand strategy, creative direction, and marketing.

### Do you work with businesses outside of Canada?

Yes. While we are based in Toronto, we serve clients across the United States, Germany, and the Middle East. We have extensive experience working remotely and across time zones.

## Working Together

### What is the typical process for a new project?

Every project begins with a discovery phase where we understand your business, market, and goals. From there, we develop a strategy, move into creative execution, and provide ongoing support. The exact process depends on the scope and type of engagement.

### How much does a typical project cost?

Project costs vary significantly based on scope, complexity, and deliverables. We provide detailed proposals after an initial discovery conversation so you know exactly what to expect. Contact us to discuss your specific needs.

### What are Precision Impact Sprints?

Precision Impact Sprints are focused, time-boxed engagements designed to deliver fast, measurable results. They are perfect for businesses that need momentum quickly without committing to a long-term retainer upfront.
