# Start Here

A new top-level section of twenty pages that meet a visitor at the need they
arrived with, in their own words, and route them to the services that answer it.

---

## 1. Why this exists

The site is organized the way the work is organized: Foundation, Execution,
Growth, Infrastructure, and 28 services beneath them. That structure is correct
and it stays. It serves the visitor who already knows what to buy.

It does not serve two other people.

**The visitor with a goal instead of a service name.** Someone thinking "we want
to grow sales" has to translate that into Lead Generation, Sales Funnel Building,
Digital Marketing and Customer Retention Marketing before the site can help.

**The visitor looking for something you do but do not advertise.** The service
pages are written as capability lists, and the capabilities inside them cannot be
found. `video-production.md` lists 16 video types. `communication-design.md`
lists 9 design disciplines. `custom-app-development.md` lists 7 application
types. That is roughly 40 real capabilities living as bullets, unreachable by
search and invisible to anyone browsing.

Packaging was not an exception to that. It was a symptom of it.

### The structural finding

**Alive Pro does AI in four separate services and has no page that says so.**
AI-Generated Production, "AI & Automation Opportunities" inside Marketing
Innovation, "AI-Powered Routing" inside Intelligent Systems Integration, and
"AI-Enhanced Insights" inside Dashboards & Analytics. The capability is real,
it is spread across four pages, and no visitor can assemble it. Against roughly
21,600 searches a month, this is the largest single gap on the site.

### The admission test

A need earns a page only when **no single existing page already answers it**,
for one of two reasons:

1. **It spans several services.** "We want to grow sales" is four services.
2. **The capability is real but has no page.** Packaging design is one bullet on
   line 30 of `communication-design.md`. Consulting has no page at all and the
   word "consultant" does not appear anywhere on the site.

A need that one service page already answers is **excluded**. Rebranding, Web
Solutions and Video Production each own a clear front door, and a Start Here page
on the same topic would only compete with it.

**Subject and destination are separate lists.** The admission test governs what a
page can be *about*. It does not restrict what a page can *link to*. Someone on
"our content is not as good as our business" should absolutely be sent to Video
Production.

## 3. Voice

**Headlines carry the need. Keywords live in the body.**

The H1 is the sentence the visitor would say out loud about their own business.
It never names a service and never uses internal vocabulary. Searched terms
appear in body copy, where they read naturally.

The page's job, in order: name the problem so they recognize themselves, say
plainly that this is solved work here, give one concrete thing they gain, then
route them.

Per `CLAUDE.md`: Canadian English, no em dashes, no filler, partner not vendor.
The first paragraph of each file is the H4 lede and stays at two to three
sentences.

**One spelling exception to manage.** Headings keep Canadian spelling
("Catalogues"), matching the collateral service page. Body copy must also carry
"catalog" naturally, because the US market outsearches Canada on that spelling
590 to 110.

## 5. Excluded, and why

**Online reputation** measures ~4,720/mo at a $95.35 CPC, the most expensive
keyword pulled. It **fails the admission test** because Reputation Management
already owns it. The correct action is not a Start Here page. It is optimizing
that existing service page for "online reputation management", the phrase people
actually type. **Logged as separate work, out of scope here.**

**Customer churn** and **competitive intelligence** are real needs with no
measurable search demand and no clear second service to pair with.

**Naming** is served by Brand Name & Identity alone.

**Urgency is not a page.** "We need help fast" is a speed, not a need, and the
person with a deadline still has one of the twenty. It belongs as a strip beneath
the index grid, and it is the natural home for Precision Impact Sprints once that
page is republished. Page 11, trade show, is the deadline-driven page that
already exists in the set.

## 6. Architecture

Follows `TEMPLATE-ANATOMY.md` and the collection-is-truth rule in `CLAUDE.md`.

### Content collection

New collection `startHere`, loaded from `content/start-here/*.md`, reusing the
shared `pageFields` schema in `src/content.config.ts` plus two additions:

```ts
const startHere = defineCollection({
  loader: glob({ pattern: '*.md', base: './content/start-here' }),
  schema: z.object({
    ...pageFields,
    /** Which index group this page sits in. */
    tier: z.enum(['situation', 'job']),
    /** Service URLs this need routes to, in display order. */
    services: z.array(z.string()),
  }),
});
```

`services` holds canonical service URLs, resolved against the `services` and
`pages` collections at build time. An unresolvable URL **fails the build** rather
than rendering a dead link. The editorial mapping stays in Markdown while titles
and descriptions still come from the collection, so renaming a service updates
every Start Here page with no content edit.

`tier` drives the two groups on the index and the two groups in the nav
accordion. Group labels live in the index route, not in content, because they
are structural headings rather than page copy.

### Routes

- `src/pages/start-here/index.astro`, two groups, numbered continuously 01 to 20
- `src/pages/start-here/[slug].astro`, rendered through `MasterPage`

Both prerendered. No new build or deploy configuration.

### Navigation

One new row prepended to `rows` in `src/lib/nav.ts`, ahead of the four sections:

```ts
{ label: 'Start Here', url: '/start-here', children: await startHerePages() }
```

Numbering is computed after filtering, so Start Here becomes 01 and the four
sections renumber to 02 through 05 automatically. `withOverview` adds the
"Start Here Overview" child, matching every other accordion row.

**Twenty children is long for one accordion.** The nav sub-menu shows Tier A
only, plus the overview row. Tier B is reachable from the index page, which is
what the overview row is for. This is the one place the menu deliberately does
not mirror the collection, and it is recorded here so it does not read as a bug.

Adding a Markdown file to `content/start-here/` still adds its route and its
index row with no code change.

### Hero videos

Add a `## Start Here` heading to `content/work/hero-videos.md`. Without it the
section falls to `## Default`, which works but gives all twenty the same
treatment.

### SEO

- `seoDescription` written at **150 to 160 characters** on all twenty. The
  existing 35 service pages sit at 44 to 60, a known gap; these do not repeat it.
- Canonical per page.
- JSON-LD `WebPage` plus `BreadcrumbList`. **Not `Service`**: these are entry
  points, not offerings, and marking them up as services would put them in
  competition with the real service pages in the same result set.
- 21 new URLs added to `SITEMAP.md`. No redirects needed, nothing moves.
